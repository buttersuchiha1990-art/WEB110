About Dio

My name is Dio and I am a pitbull. I am just over a year old so I am still a baby. I am full of energy and need lots of exercise. I love to play with everything. People and pets are my best friends. Since my dad works at a daycare, I go to work with him and get to play with the other dogs there. I love that and I take a good nap when I get home.




My favorite treats

  • Anything peanut butter
  • Yogurt bites
  • Bananas
  • Apple slices

Likes and Dislikes

I am a happy go lucky guy and am intrigued by many things. Here are a couple of my favorite things. I love going to daycare with my dad. I get to play with so many dogs and toys. I really love the toys the most. I am obsessed with them and I try and sneak them inside. I love going on evening walks with my mom. We go almost every night. I like to travel with my parents and explore new places. I don't mind the car rides I can pretty much sleep anywhere.


There aren't many things that I really don't like but water is a big one. I am not a fan of the pools at daycare and I won't even take a toy out of the water. Sprinklers also scare me when my dad waters the grass and flowers. Baths are also a horrible time. I hate getting in the tub and getting shampooed.
