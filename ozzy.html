About Ozzy

My name is Ozzy and I am a miniature schnauzer. I am thirteen years old and I love to sleep. I play a little bit with my brother but he can be a bit much. I am kind of grumpy with dogs I don't know so I would not like going to daycare. I stay home and sit in my recliner or watch out the window from on top of the couch.

My favorite treats

  1. Yogurt bites
  2. Apple slices
  3. Bananas
  4. Jerky snacks

Likes and Dislikes

I don't have a favorite toy since I have had many in my lifetime but I get really excited when we get a new toy. I like to go on walks when the weather is cool enough for me. I love to sit in the recliner and try to trick people out of it so I can lay there. I love to spend time with my mom and dad. I like to lay on the couch with my dad and sleep in bed with my mom. I like when we travel to new places. I get to explore new things. I have a backpack my dad wears for me to get in when I get to tired on trips.


I am kind of grumpy and don't like being told what to do. I hate getting my beard and teeth brushed. I like my brother but don't like when he tries to cuddle to close. I don't really care for getting a bath or going to the groomer. Although I do feel a lot better after a good haircut. I don't like being hot.
